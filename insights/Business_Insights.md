# Business Insights --- Financial Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **700 transactions** from the Microsoft Financial
Sample for 2010.

-   **Total Sales:** \$118.73M
-   **Total Profit:** \$18.45M
-   **Total Profit Margin:** 15.54%
-   **Total COGS:** \$101.83M
-   **Total Discounts:** \$9.21M
-   **Total Units Sold:** 1.13M

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Paseo was the highest-performing product by sales and profit

-   **Sales:** \$33.01M
-   **Profit:** \$5.04M
-   **Profit Margin:** 15.26%

Paseo generated the highest sales and profit among the six products
analyzed, making it the largest product-level contributor to overall
financial performance.

### 2. Government was the largest segment by sales and profit

-   **Sales:** \$52.50M
-   **Profit:** \$11.39M
-   **Profit Margin:** 21.69%

The Government segment generated the largest sales and profit
contribution among the analyzed segments.

### 3. Channel Partners recorded the highest profit margin

-   **Sales:** \$1.80M
-   **Profit:** \$1.32M
-   **Profit Margin:** 73.13%

Channel Partners recorded the highest segment-level profit margin. Its
revenue contribution was substantially smaller than the larger segments,
so the margin should be considered alongside its sales scale.

### 4. France generated the highest absolute country-level profit

-   **Sales:** \$24.35M
-   **Profit:** \$4.03M
-   **Profit Margin:** 16.56%

France generated the highest total profit among the five countries
analyzed.

Germany had a slightly higher profit margin at **16.85%**, compared with
France's **16.56%**.

### 5. Higher discount bands were associated with lower observed profit margins

  Discount Band        Sales   Profit Margin
  --------------- ---------- ---------------
  No Discount        \$7.94M          21.86%
  Low               \$34.63M          17.87%
  Medium            \$38.78M          15.19%
  High              \$37.37M          12.40%

The analysis shows progressively lower observed profit margins across
higher discount bands.

**Important:** This is an association in the analyzed data, not proof
that discounts alone caused the lower margins. Product, country,
segment, and other factors may also affect profitability.

### 6. October recorded the strongest monthly sales and profit in the analyzed period

**October 2010**

-   **Sales:** \$21.67M
-   **Profit:** \$3.68M
-   **Units Sold:** 201,104

October recorded the strongest monthly sales and profit among the
analyzed monthly results.

The dataset ends on **December 2, 2010**, so December represents only a
partial month and should not be compared with full months without
qualification.

## Executive Summary

The Financial Performance Analytics project analyzes 700 transactions
across products, countries, customer segments, and discount bands. Total
sales reached \$118.73M with \$18.45M in profit and a 15.54% overall
profit margin. Paseo was the highest-selling product, while the
Government segment generated the largest sales and profit contribution.
Channel Partners recorded the highest segment-level profit margin at
73.13%, although its revenue contribution was relatively small. France
generated the highest absolute country-level profit at \$4.03M, while
Germany had a slightly higher profit margin. The discount analysis shows
progressively lower observed margins as discount levels increase, and
October recorded the strongest monthly sales and profit in the analyzed
period.

## Portfolio Takeaways

1.  **Product:** Paseo --- highest sales and profit.
2.  **Segment:** Government --- largest sales and profit contribution.
3.  **Margin:** Channel Partners --- highest segment-level margin.
4.  **Country:** France --- highest absolute profit.
5.  **Discount:** Higher discount bands --- lower observed profit
    margins.
6.  **Time:** October --- strongest analyzed monthly sales and profit.
